## Important corrections

- **Two restarts:** October 15 from stable v1.2, then October 17 from stable 3.5.3. Current stable is 3.6.1;
  frozen v1.4 is 3.6. Experimental's engine update was authored October 21 and merged October 22 in
  [PR #80](https://github.com/ghempy53/deadlock-config/pull/80), adopting the October 20 Valve patch.
- **Owner choices:** DX11 and uncapped FPS remain the baseline. Both files request mip bias 2, texture pool 6400 MB,
  bilinear filtering, no explicit resolution cap, Steam Input off, and disabled minimum-particle-timestep overrides.
  PR #84 restores the disabled stable aspect-ratio override after PR #83; both files leave it disabled for the
  owner's preferred FOV mod slider. PR #83's Sandbox cap report remains owner-reported historical context.
  Earlier full-resolution/8x texture and cross-renderer promises are superseded.
- **Tests:** October 6–7 results were invalidated by the owner due to PC problems. The October 17 particle bisect is
  a distinct owner-reported visual result; it does not establish FPS gains, an exact CPU cost or every later build's behavior.
- **Defaults versus requests:** registered defaults, stock overrides, upstream values and file occurrences are now
  separate. For example, `fps_max` is declared 120, stock requests 400 and both configs request 0. A disabled line
  makes no assignment; it does not reset other configuration layers.
- **Damage numbers and cameras:** only two owner lifetime/emphasis tweaks were removed. An inherited damage-text
  batching line remains. Stable/experimental vertical camera flattening is true/false, and highlight speeds are
  999/100. Claims of all-default damage numbers or identical camera configuration were incorrect.
- **Precedence and restrictions:** no public evidence inspected proves last-wins duplicate behavior, complete saved
  settings/menu order or blanket cheat/development startup acceptance. Replication and server-module registration
  constrain conclusions about online behavior. The reference retains all occurrences and flags.
- **Missing names:** 48 of the 531 distinct inventoried ConVars are absent from this dump, including disabled examples.
  Several absent names still occur in the same stock file. “Absent” must not be rewritten as “removed” or “harmless.”
- **Blocked and out-of-range:** stable has 15 active flagged names, experimental 16; both have four out-of-range
  requests. The dump declares limits, not observed clamp behavior. The full blocked inventory is
  [124 names](../BLOCKED-CONVARS.md). These entries remain unchanged for owner-directed review.
- **Engine/tooling scope:** both configs retain four edited guarded sections; experimental is not a stock fallback.
  `PGIVersion` matches but matchmaking is untested. Compiler/editor defaults do not rebake shipped assets. Memory
  estimates are not allocation limits; internal HDR buffers are not monitor HDR. Several added keys have no verified loader.
- **Performance claims:** fixed particle seeds are a debugging feature, size-cull thresholds trade CPU culling for
  drawing, and disabling SIMD is not automatically faster. Texture streaming involves more than VRAM; a larger JS
  heap limit is not a cache. Detailed corrections are in the subsystem reports.
